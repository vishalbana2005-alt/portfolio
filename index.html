about

From civil engineering to civil-sized datasets

I spent four years training as a civil engineer and came out the other side more interested in the data behind decisions than the structures themselves — demand patterns, growth curves, churn signals. That shift is now the through-line of my work: I like problems where a number on a dashboard changes what a team does next.

My work splits across three lanes that feed each other: data analysis (SQL, Python, Power BI) to answer business questions, product thinking (won PMx at IIT Guwahati for product strategy) to ask the right ones, and data engineering (Airflow, dbt, Docker, Snowflake) to make sure the answers don't break the next morning.
